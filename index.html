I'm a PhD candidate at Rutgers Philosophy. I am interested in epistemic questions with political implications: my published work is on higher-order uncertainty and belief polarization, and my current research is about how rational agents should revise their beliefs when they revise their concepts. I also have research interests in the philosophy of computing and in Chinese Philosophy, especially 莊子.

I joined Rutgers in 2022. From 2020-2022 I worked at Placer County Superior Court in Roseville and the California Supreme Court in San Francisco. Before that I did my undergraduate studies at Stanford. I grew up just east of Interstate 5 in Northern San Diego in Southern California on the U.S. West Coast.
